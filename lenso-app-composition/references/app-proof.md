# App Proof

App Proof should identify the exact generated-state revision and demonstrate:

- the expected Modules and Services are present;
- required configuration and installation actions are accounted for;
- one business workflow runs through the real host boundary;
- the focused checks for changed capabilities pass; and
- the configured Console Service shows the expected operational evidence.

Use the installed CLI's current verification command and proof format. A proof
is stale when the generated state or relevant implementation changes after it
was written.
