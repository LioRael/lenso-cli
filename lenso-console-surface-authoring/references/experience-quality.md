# Experience quality

Design around the operator's decision and next action. Establish a clear page
title, current Managed Service, scope, primary status, and the evidence needed
to act. Prefer progressive detail over a grid of unrelated dashboard cards.

Cover the complete state model: loading, empty, partial, stale, error, denied,
pending, success, and destructive confirmation. Preserve useful context during
refreshes and expose the difference between no data and unavailable data.

Verify narrow and wide layouts, light and dark themes, internal scrolling,
long labels and values, keyboard traversal, visible focus, Escape behavior,
reduced motion, accessible names, and localized labels. Motion should explain
state or spatial change and must not block input.

Use shared patterns for page spacing, typography, controls, inspectors,
drawers, tables, and feedback. Introduce a new visual pattern only when the
operator workflow cannot be expressed by an existing one.
