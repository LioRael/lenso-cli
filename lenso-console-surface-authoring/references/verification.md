# Console Surface verification

Account for every changed Surface across these layers:

1. **Declaration:** typed Module declaration and generated manifest agree.
2. **Contract:** protocol, identity, paths, navigation, capabilities, and
   presentation validate.
3. **Package:** lint, typecheck, focused component tests, and production build
   pass in the owning UI repository.
4. **Artifact:** entry, styles, digests, compatibility, receipt, and Module
   Release match.
5. **Loader:** the valid artifact loads and representative invalid artifacts
   enter quarantine without disabling the backend Module.
6. **Authority:** read-only access, denied mutation, reauthorization, and
   unavailable Managed Service behavior are correct.
7. **Isolation:** switching Managed Service context cannot leak records,
   capabilities, actions, configuration, inventory, or pending results.
8. **Browser:** light and dark themes, wide and narrow viewports, internal
   scrolling, keyboard, focus, Escape, and reduced motion are exercised.

Static screenshots do not prove interaction or scrolling. Record the route,
viewport, theme, state, action, and observable result for browser evidence.
