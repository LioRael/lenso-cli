# Evidence and verification

Collect producer and Consumer versions, Contract and binding digests, generated
client revisions, deployment observations, usage or discovery evidence,
deprecation timestamps, replacement readiness, and rollback inputs.

Verify source-to-artifact freshness, positive and negative contract vectors,
old and new clients, mixed versions, authentication and context, error
envelopes, retry and deadline semantics, and deterministic cleanup.

Treat missing or stale Consumer evidence as unknown, not zero Consumers. A
retirement plan becomes stale when any bound evidence or supported component
changes.
