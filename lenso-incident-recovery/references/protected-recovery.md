# Protected recovery

Backup restore, destructive cleanup, regional authority cutover, failback,
trust or identity changes, policy bypass, Workflow termination, and business
compensation cross an Approval Boundary.

Prepare a stale-safe plan that identifies exact inputs, authority before and
after, completed effects, excluded sessions or writers, fencing, success and
abort conditions, rollback limits, observation window, and cleanup. Stop until
named approval is bound to the current digest.

After execution, verify both data and authority. A healthy process with stale
authority, duplicated effects, unfenced writers, or incomplete cleanup is not
a recovered system.
