# Blockers and Approval Boundary

Block extraction when ownership is unclear, another Module reads or writes the
candidate tables, a local transaction would be split without a declared
replacement, Contract Consumers are unknown, stable record identity is absent,
or the candidate cannot be reconciled and rolled back.

Backfill, verification, and provisional routing remain preparation while the
linked Module is authoritative. Authority transfer changes writes, routing,
recovery, and business responsibility together. Bind approval to the exact
plan and fresh evidence digest; any changed input requires a new review.

Destructive cleanup follows proven authority and rollback-window policy. It is
never an implicit final extraction step.
